# Regulatory sources

Approval records and standards that name the four titles. All read on **2026-10-13**.

**How to read the "Checked" column.** *twice* means the entry was read in the primary document in two
independent passes and is labelled VERIFIED. *once* means a single reading that has not been
re-checked; it is labelled STRONG EVIDENCE until it is.

None of these documents states an RTP, a line count, a denomination list or a pay table for Dragon Link. A US regulator's list does print a payout range for two of the titles, and the ACT printed Lightning Link's return by denomination in 2015: see [PUBLISHED_RETURNS.md](PUBLISHED_RETURNS.md).

## The national standard

*Australian/New Zealand Gaming Machine National Standard*, revision 12.1 (May 2025):
<https://www.vgccc.vic.gov.au/sites/default/files/2026-01/Gaming-Machine-National-Standard-V12.1.pdf>.
Read once.

- It sets a minimum return to player per jurisdiction as a parameter, and limits how much RTP may
  vary across betting options.
- It requires the machine itself to display game information, including the RTP or its range and
  the linked-jackpot contribution (clauses 4.50 to 4.52).

The second point matters for this project: **the machine's own information screen is the most
direct lawful source of the RTP and bet figures that no published document gives.** A recording of
that screen on an Australian machine would turn several unknowns into verified claims.

Minimum-RTP figures per jurisdiction were extracted from a multi-column table in that document and
have not been checked against the PDF by eye; they are not reproduced here until they are.
