# What public footage shows

Findings read from public recordings of machines being played, for all four titles. Golden Century
has [its own section](#golden-century); the sections before it cover Happy & Prosperous, Panda Magic
and Autumn Moon. Read 2026-10-13.

The recordings are source material, kept privately with their hashes; this page holds what was read
from them, with the video and timestamp for each item. Video references are YouTube identifiers:
`youtube.com/watch?v=<id>`.

## How this was done, and its limits

- About 150 to 175 videos per title were scanned through their preview storyboards (one small
  frame every 5 to 10 seconds), filtered for text-page screens, and the candidates read at full
  resolution. **This is not frame by frame**: a screen shown for under about five seconds can be
  missed.
- **No Australian recording of a Game Rules, pay-table or information screen was found** for any of
  the four titles. No return figure and no version identifier was found anywhere.
- The **US** rules and pay-table screens were found and transcribed for three titles. For Golden
  Century none was found in any country.
- No recording could be placed against a regulatory specification: no frame shows a version, and
  every Australian top-screen strip from 2018 to 2026 reads "© 2016".
- Labels: VERIFIED here means legible in a frame and confirmed in a second frame or recording.
  STRONG EVIDENCE means legible in one frame. Scope is stated for every item: US footage is not
  evidence about the Australian build.

## Three cautions for anyone reading footage

1. **Titles mislabel the product.** Several clips titled "Dragon Link" show, on the machine's own
   top-screen strip, the stand-alone sibling **Dragon Cash**. Their line and bet configurations
   must not be used for Dragon Link. Identify the product from the strip, not the title.
2. **There are at least two Australian presentations.** Queensland club machines show "N credits
   per line", a games menu and an information button, with denominations 1c to $1. Casino machines
   show "Bet Multiplier N", a minimum bet and a Game Rules button, and one shows denominations from
   5c to $2.
3. **The US rules pages say "50 lines are played". Australian club machines do not** play 50 lines
   at every denomination.

## Hold & Spin (US rules text)

Read on the Happy & Prosperous rules pages (`t_g9hlCODBU` 4:14.0, 4:16.5; `OxMnVxG6Yqc` 0:15.2),
and matching on Autumn Moon's at reduced size. VERIFIED for the US. In our words:

- Six to fourteen scattered prize symbols trigger the feature. The triggering symbols are held and
  the other positions become **individual reels**. Three free spins are awarded, and the count
  returns to three whenever a new prize symbol lands.
- The feature ends when no spins remain or the Grand is won. Prizes are awarded at the end.
  Pay-table prizes are not awarded during it. The rules state that bonus reels are in play.
- A prize symbol carries Major, Minor, Mini, or one of twelve multiples of the total bet:
  250, 200, 100, 50, 20, 15, 10, 5, 4, 3, 2, 1. **Only one prize symbol may hold 250, 200 or Major.**
- Jackpot wording: "linked progressive Grand jackpot", "stand-alone progressive Major jackpot";
  the Minor is 5,000 credits and the Mini 1,000 credits.

In Australia (VERIFIED as displayed, `XP7TKGqq7zM` 1:48 to 2:23 and others): the prompt that six
prize symbols start the feature; a "free spins remaining" counter; a counting "WINNER" meter on the
top screen; prize symbols showing credits at 1c to 10c and dollars at $1.

## Jackpots as displayed in Australia

- The four levels are labelled Grand Jackpot, Major Jackpot, Minor Bonus, Mini Bonus. VERIFIED.
- The Grand and Major show values that are not round and differ between venues; the Major rose
  within one session (`eKnm_PT_Zyg` 3:20 against 10:00). The Minor and Mini are round and scale with
  the denomination. STRONG EVIDENCE that the upper two move and the lower two are fixed. Whether
  each is linked or stand-alone is stated on the US rules page and on no Australian screen seen.
- Minor and Mini on club machines: $50 and $10 at 1c; $100 and $20 at 2c; $500 and $100 at 10c;
  $1,250 and $250 at $1. On a casino machine: $5,000 and $1,000 at $1.

## Other

- Autumn Moon, on an Australian casino machine, offers a "Lucky Chance Spin": when remaining
  credits are less than the current bet, the player may stake them for a chance at one game at that
  bet (`2P7J7tM63A8` 12:45). STRONG EVIDENCE.

What this adds to the other three titles' findings:

- **Lines are chosen by the player**, up to a maximum that depends on the denomination. The table
  near the top of this page gives those maximums. The side line indicators lag a denomination change
  by about a second, so only frames where lines, credits per line and denomination multiply to the
  displayed bet were used.
- **In every denomination the Mini is 1,000 credits and the Minor 5,000 credits**, except at $1 and
  $2 where they are 250 and 1,250 credits. That resolves the second conflict below as a rule of the
  club configuration rather than an inconsistency.
- **Hold & Spin discs can carry the Mini or the Major** by name, as the US rules text says.

**Dragon Cash, again.** "Golden Century - Dragon Cash" has the same main screen. Every clip read
from one NSW channel, and two 2026 clips from the main Queensland channel, are Dragon Cash by their
own top-screen line. Its NSW machine offers 1c to $2 and a 30-line option at 1c. It is a separate

**Generations cannot be told apart.** Every legible Australian product line from August 2019 to
October 2026 reads "© 2016", and the denominations, layout, prompts and Mini and Minor values are
unchanged across that span. Nothing recognisable as the "Grand" variant appears.

**A way to the pay table that needs no rules screen.** The machine prints each line win on a status
line. Read from a session played on one line, or with the line map known, those messages give the
pay table directly. A full-resolution session is held for it; automatic text recognition did not
read the small text reliably, so it is a manual job. No single-line Dragon Link session was found.

## Conflicts recorded

1. **Lines:** 50 at every denomination on US rules pages; 50, 25 or 5 by denomination on Australian
   club machines; 50 at 10c on an Australian casino machine.
2. **Minor and Mini at $1:** 5,000 and 1,000 credits by the US rules text and on the casino
   machine; $1,250 and $250 on club machines.
3. **"Up to 250x on a scatter"** on the manufacturer's pages does not match the screens: the
   scatter tops out at 100 times the total bet. 250 is the largest prize-symbol multiple, and in
   Autumn Moon the wild's top free-games line pay.
4. **Retrigger size:** three for two titles; six in the one Autumn Moon sample.
