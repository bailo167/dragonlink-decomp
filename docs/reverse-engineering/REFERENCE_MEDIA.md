## Using footage

1. Identify what is shown first: title, market, cabinet, denomination, bet, and the build if a
   version is visible. Footage of a US machine is not evidence about the Australian release.
2. Prefer segments that show the **rules, pay-table and game-information screens**: those are
   player-visible facts and can be recorded as VERIFIED for the build shown.
3. Timing measurements are bounded by the recording's frame rate; state the bound.
4. A session's outcomes are a small sample. They can validate a recovered model; they cannot
   establish a probability ([math/README.md](math/README.md), "Role of gameplay captures").
5. The owner's own recordings on Australian machines are better evidence than any of the above,
   because their provenance is known.
